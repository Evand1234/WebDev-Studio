The story (as you scroll)

The phone stays still in the middle of the screen. Inside it, the aircon site plays out while the visitor's thoughts pop up next to it.

1“Aircon's dead. It's 34°.”They find the company on Google.
2“Come on…”Slow loading. A timer counts up.
3“I don't want specials.”The pop-up gets in the way.
4“Do they even do Midrand?”Walls of text.
5“Where's the number?”Still no price, no button.
6“Next one.”They leave.
My pick
like this: phone with pop-up cards

APhone stays, thoughts pop up

Smooth and easy to follow. Feelings without long text.

like this: grows to full screen

BSite fills your screen

You're stuck in the bad site yourself for a moment. Harder to read on a phone.

like this: stacking cards

CCards that stack

Each frustration is a card piling up. Feels more like slides.

Why I picked it: one phone that stays still is the smoothest, and the pop-up thoughts tell the story without paragraphs.

The plan: the page from top to bottom

This is what I want to build if you go with my picks. One long page, no separate pages, black with orange.

  1. 1 · TopCalm intro

    What you seeBlack page, soft orange glow, “They see your site first.” and a WhatsApp button.

    What movesThe headline rises in line by line. On scroll, the whole top shrinks into a phone.

  2. 2 · Their siteThe average aircon site

    What you seeThe phone shows Arctic Breeze: pop-up, grey text, photos at the bottom.

    What movesThe page loads slowly, the pop-up lands, the page scrolls on its own. Thought bubbles pop up beside the phone. A timer counts up.

  3. 3 · The switchRelief

    What you see“Now picture it like this.”

    What movesAn orange wave sweeps up and washes the grey site away.

  4. 4 · Our sideSame business, rebuilt

    What you seeThe aircon site, now beautiful: photo, one line, WhatsApp button. Then a café, shop, salon and guesthouse.

    What movesOur version slides into the phone. The row of phones slides sideways as you scroll down.

  5. 5 · How it worksFour steps

    What you seeCall, design, build, launch. 50% to start, 1–2 weeks.

    What movesThe current step lights up as you scroll.

  6. 6 · PricesPackages and care

    What you seeThree dark cards, Professional glows orange. A switch for monthly care.

    What movesCards rise in one after another.

  7. 7 · QuestionsFAQ

    What you seeSix short questions.

    What movesSmooth open and close.

  8. 8 · EndLet's build yours

    What you seeGlowing card, WhatsApp button, BrandMasters huge underneath.

    What movesThe glow drifts slowly. The big name slides up into place.

Everywhere: smooth scrolling, everything slides and fades gently (never jumpy), and it's checked on a phone-sized screen before you see it.
