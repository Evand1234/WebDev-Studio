The page, top to bottom

One long scroll, no separate pages. Each moving picture shows roughly what that part does.

01Calm top

A soft orange glow and one short line: “They see your site first.” Quiet, so the story below gets the attention.

Effects: glow slowly breathes, words rise in one by one.

Where's the price?Still loading…

02Their site

As you scroll, the glow shrinks into a phone showing an ordinary aircon company site. Walls of grey text, photos dumped at the bottom. It tried, it's just tiring.

Effects: little thought bubbles pop up with how a visitor feels. A timer counts up to 1:52 to find the phone number.

Now picture it like this.

03The switch

A warm orange wave rises up and washes over the old site.

Effects: one big smooth sweep. This is the main “wow” moment.

04Same business, rebuilt

The same aircon company, now dark and clean with a clear WhatsApp button.

Effects: the timer now says 0:08. The pieces slide into place.

05Any business

A row of phones slides sideways: a café, a shop, a salon, a guesthouse. Shows we're for everyone.

Effects: the row moves as you scroll down.

06How it works

Three or four simple steps: we chat, we design, you check it, it goes live.

Effects: each step lights up as you reach it.

07Prices

Essential, Professional and Premium side by side. Professional glows as the recommended one. Monthly care underneath.

Effects: cards tilt up into place.

08Questions and ending

A few short questions that open when tapped, then a glowing card: “Let's build yours.” with WhatsApp. A giant faint name at the very bottom.

Effects: glow follows your finger or mouse a little.
