Privacy · POPIA

Your information, handled simply.

This page explains how BrandMasters looks after personal information, in line with South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA). Last updated 19 October 2026.

Who we are

BrandMasters is a web design studio in Johannesburg, South Africa. You can reach us on WhatsApp at 081 089 8573.

What we collect

Only what you choose to send us on WhatsApp, for example your name, your business name and your number. This website has no forms that store data and no advertising trackers.

Why we collect it

To reply to you, understand your business and send you a quote or a preview of your site.

Who we share it with

Nobody. We do not sell your information and we do not share it with third parties.

How long we keep it

For as long as your project needs it, or until you ask us to delete it.

Your rights

Under POPIA you can:

  • ask what information we hold about you,
  • ask us to correct it, and
  • ask us to delete it.

Message us on WhatsApp at 081 089 8573 and we will deal with your request.

Complaints

If you are unhappy with how we handled your information you can complain to the Information Regulator of South Africa at inforegulator.org.za.
