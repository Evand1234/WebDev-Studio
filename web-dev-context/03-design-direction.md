# Design direction (current, set 18 Oct 2026)

## The direction in Evan's words
"We must just stick to the basics of modernization. Like, what everybody else is creating, that's what we must also create... I don't want to go over the top. Just create a nice, clean site that's premium and good. We don't have to create a new meta for website creation."

So: **clean, premium, standard modern design**, the kind good studios and top SaaS/agency sites ship today. Not experimental, not a reinvention of web design. Polish and smoothness over novelty.

**This replaces older direction.** Earlier briefs pushed heavy "new meta" ideas: crumpling the old site into a box, a 20-minute timer, a patience meter, black marble with a torch cursor, a pinned stage of colour-world chapters, transition maps for every scene. Don't build those unless Evan asks for them again.

## What the BrandMasters site must have
1. **A clear hero.** In one look: we build websites for businesses like yours. Short headline, one sentence, two buttons (WhatsApp, and see how it works). A realistic phone (and maybe a laptop) showing a great site. Calm, confident, no gimmicks that distract.
2. **The showcase: plays like a video, never feels like a video.** As you scroll, it just runs: a business's weak site turns into a great one, step by step, on a realistic phone and laptop. Smooth, scroll-driven, reversible, never a `<video>` with controls, never a player look, never stutters. One clear idea per step. Think Apple product pages: the device stays put and its screen changes as you scroll.
3. **What we do / how it works** (call, design, build, launch; 50% deposit, 1 to 2 weeks, 50% before launch).
4. **Proof.** TappedFlow as real work; anything else labelled as a concept.
5. **Packages and care plans**, calm and clear, Professional highlighted, each with a pre-filled WhatsApp button.
6. **FAQ** (how long, what do you need from me, do you write the words, can I update it myself, what does care cover, how does payment work). Answer only from the facts in `01-business.md`.
7. **Final call to action and footer**: WhatsApp, call, email buttons; logo, slogan, "Johannesburg, South Africa".

One continuous scrolling page. Evan hates the feeling of separate "pages", so no multi-page site for BrandMasters, no hard section breaks. Sections should flow into each other.

## The bad site and the story (Evan, 18 Oct 2026, he has said this many times)
- **The "before" site is a normal person's site, not a disaster.** Model it on the look of the reference venue screenshot: a big centred title in a slightly dated font and colour, then paragraph after paragraph of centred grey text that says everything at once, then a strip of nice-enough photos dumped at the bottom over a faded background photo, where they don't belong. The owner tried a little. Nothing is broken, nothing is a joke, no pop-up circus. It's just tiring and forgettable.
- **No trades as the example.** No barbers, plumbers or similar as the demo business. Keep it a generic "online business site" look, so any owner sees their own site in it.
- **The story is about feelings.** As you scroll, compare how a visitor feels on their site (lost, skimming, leaving) with how they feel on ours (clear, welcome, one tap to act). Told by scrolling, not a video.
- Never use the reference venue's name, text or photos. Write our own text in the same style.

## Taste (still true)
- Minimal text. Pictures and motion carry the message; words label what the picture already shows. Headlines 3 to 6 words.
- Everything earns its place. No filler sections, no pointless widgets.
- Welcoming to any business owner (a plumber, a salon, a guesthouse). Not techy, not "only for people who work online".
- Never empty: no grey placeholder boxes, no text-only screens, no tiny picture inside a device frame.
- Copy: natural, confident English with contractions. No buzzwords (seamless, leverage, unlock, game-changer, cutting-edge). No "it's not X, it's Y".

## Phone standard (Evan asked for this specifically)
- Realistic **iPhone 15 Pro**-style frame: proportions about 1 to 2.03, thin titanium rim, dynamic island, status bar, home indicator.
- The screen is **completely filled by a real website designed for that screen**, with real photos. Never a screenshot shrunk inside the frame, never picture-in-picture, never overlapping or cut-off text.
- Same rule for laptops and browser frames.
- Build it once as a reusable component and reuse it everywhere.

## Creativity
Be creative inside this direction: pick the best references, combine them well, sweat the details (spacing, type, easing, image quality). The creativity is in the craft and in fitting the site to the business, not in inventing new kinds of interaction. Research freely (21st.dev, Dribbble, Awwwards, Godly, Land-book, top studio sites), take the principle behind what you like, never copy a design.

## Client sites (later)
Same standard. Each client site is built around that business (its photos, its reviews, its one main action: call, book, or quote). Demos are built to Professional level.
