# BrandMasters: the business

## What it is
**BrandMasters** (brandmasters.co.za) is a web design studio in Johannesburg, South Africa. We build websites for South African businesses of every kind (trades, restaurants, salons, clinics, guesthouses, shops, gyms, offices) that have **strong Google reviews but a weak website**. The owners aren't technical and mostly look at things on their phones.

- Slogan: **"They see your site first."** Customers judge a business by its website before they ever call.
- The one message: **We build websites for you. Already have one? We learn from it and build it better.**
- The visitor's question: "Why do I need a better website, and what will it do for me?" The site answers that by showing, not by leading with price.
- We speak as a studio ("we"). The brand is BrandMasters.
