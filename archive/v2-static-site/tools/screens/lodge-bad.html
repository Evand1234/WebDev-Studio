Welcome to Signal View Guest House

Signal View Guest House is a guest house and also a conference venue and also offers tours, weddings, functions, birthdays, teambuilding, and many other services for the discerning traveller and business person alike, situated in a quiet yet central location which is close to many things that you may wish to see or do while you are visiting the Cape Town area for business or for leisure or both.

About our history and the history of the area

The history of the building goes back many years to when the original owners, who were of the opinion that a house of this size ought to be used for the benefit of travellers, first opened its doors, after which it changed hands several times before being acquired by the present management, who have since renovated certain rooms (not all).
